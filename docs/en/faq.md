# FAQ

**Does Wilco replace Pi?**
No. Pi runs on your Mac exactly as before. Wilco is a remote control for it.

**Do I need an account?**
No. You pair your phone with your Mac directly by scanning a QR code.

**Does my Mac need to stay on?**
Yes. Pi runs on your Mac, so the Mac has to be awake and online for you to reach it. Wilco Host can keep the Mac from sleeping while a session is running.

**Can I keep using Pi in the terminal?**
Yes. Install `wilco-bridge` and your phone can follow and drive the session you started in the terminal. Only one side writes to a session at a time.

**Which Pi versions are supported?**
The Mac host checks the installed Pi version and runs a compatibility check after every Pi update. Supported versions will be listed with each release.

**Why is the iOS app not open source while the relay is?**
The parts you might need to run or audit yourself (the relay, the Pi extensions and the protocol) are open. The apps are distributed as signed binaries.

**What does "Wilco" mean?**
It is aviation radio shorthand for "will comply": the instruction was received and will be carried out.
