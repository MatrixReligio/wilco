# Code of Conduct

Wilco adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Wilco to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the [MatrixReligio GitHub organization](https://github.com/MatrixReligio). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies and is incorporated by reference.
